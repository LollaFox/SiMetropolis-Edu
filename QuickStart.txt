1. Get the Mac or Windows download from README.md.
2. Extract the ZIP locally and double-click Open SiMetropolis Edu. If setup is interrupted, reconnect and select Retry.
3. Choose the opening language and click Start class in the teacher tab. On the classroom network, select Project join information for your groups.
4. Keep the launcher running. If you close the teacher tab, double-click Open SiMetropolis Edu again from the same folder.
